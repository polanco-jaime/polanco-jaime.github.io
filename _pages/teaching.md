## Teaching Philosophy

<div style="margin-bottom: 20px; padding: 15px; border: 1px solid #ddd; border-radius: 8px;">
<p class="text-justify" style="font-size: 0.95rem;">
I believe in learning by doing. My courses combine theoretical foundations with computational practice, encouraging students to engage with real data from the very first class. I integrate programming tools (Python, R, Stata) into the curriculum so that students develop practical skills alongside their statistical intuition. I am also committed to leveraging AI-powered educational tools to personalize learning and improve student outcomes, an area I actively research.
</p>
</div>
